    How the three games fit together

    Everyone plays the same visit, but in a different game. When the tank keeps Sunny delighted, the foragers' paths grow wide. When foragers bring nectar home, the healer gets honey for more salves. When the healer's salves land, the tank's timing gets easier and the foragers can keep flying.

    Nobody gets hurt, ever. A bee who gets too sneezy rests on a leaf for a moment, and the nectar you carry home always counts.
